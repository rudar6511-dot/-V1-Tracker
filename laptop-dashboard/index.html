Privacy & limits

A PWA is installable but not physically locked to one laptop. Anyone who can open the public hosted page may install its shell; location access must be protected by server-side account ownership checks. Keep your laptop account, password, and Secret Key private.

The phone must be powered on, online, and have visible location sharing enabled for a fresh update. This app shell can open offline, but login and live location need internet.
